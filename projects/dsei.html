DSEI - Digital Security Education Initiative

This project focuses on cybersecurity education, threat analysis, and real-world case studies. The goal is to explain complex topics in a simple and educational way.

Topics Covered

  • Secure Passwords
  • Phishing
  • Privacy on Social Media
  • Social Engineering
  • How Hackers Operate
  • Malware
  • Public WiFi Security
  • Personal Data Protection
  • Cyberbullying
  • Digital Identity
  • Platform Security

Project Objective

DSEI aims to build awareness about digital threats and promote responsible and secure technology use.
